TIMERA is a three-year programme to build African capacity in
infectious-disease modelling, so that researchers and public health
professionals based in Africa are better equipped to prepare for and respond to
outbreaks and pandemics.

The programme will train about 50 fellows from national public health
institutes and research centres across Africa. It offers short courses in
advanced epidemiological modelling, including AI-supported approaches, and
combines classroom and online teaching with field-based practicums, mentorship,
policy communication and public health data leadership. TIMERA builds on the
networks of the Field Epidemiology and Laboratory Training Programmes (FELTP).

At Radboudumc, we contribute expertise in biostatistics and mathematical
modelling to the design and delivery of the training programme.
